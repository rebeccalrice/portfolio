UX/UI CASE STUDY - 2026

PokeStop

One app to find, compare, buy and sell Pokemon Cards.

Role
Solo Designer
Tools
Figma
Project
Build an App
Timeline
Fall 2026

01 - The problem

Too many sites. Too much information.

Collectors check several sites to find the best price on a card, and the biggest one, TCGPlayer, packs in so much information that it’s hard to read at a glance. The brief asked for a live market tracker that alerts users to deals without feeling like spam.

02 - Research

What the market gets right, and wrong.

I focused on Pokémon cards only, and on enthusiasts rather than scalpers.

TCGPlayer

The Most data, but hard to scan.

StockX

Far easier to read at a glance.

Collectr

Bare-bones, but I want to build on it.

eBay

Everyone knows eBay, a great entry point for sellers.

03 - Meet Kay

The Collector

Kay, 24, collects her favorite older cards and is hunting a specific Porygon. She wants one app, one purchase, and information she can read at a glance.

No off-site buying Easy Filters Clear selling Info

04 - The flow, and the detour

Even with a detour, Kay never leaves the app.

Kay gets an alert, compares sellers, and checks out. But when she doesn’t have enough money, she sells her Rowlet inside the app, then buys the next cheapest Porygon.

Happy path

Get price alert Open Watchlist Compare 4 sellers Check out

The detour

Not enough money List Rowlet Rowlet Sells Cheapest card gone Buy next cheapest Checked out

07 - Big moment

Alerts that aren’t spam

Alerts only fire for cards on Kay’s watchlist, at a price she set. If she gets a notification, it’s one she asked for.

PokestopA card you want has appeared for the desired price!

PokestopYour Rowlet (082/080) has sold for $5.93!

PokestopError! Not enough money in that account.

09 - Feedback

What critique changed

BEFORE

Dark blue, contrasts poorly against the background.

AFTER

Lighter blue stands out against the darker interface.

Phone UI

Adding device details made the screens read as a real app.

Friction Point

Critique helped me find the money detour. It took a few rounds, but it became the center of the flow.

10 - What’s next

I’d love to build this out as a full app one day, come back to this project after school, testing it with real collectors, possibly expanding beyond Pokémon.
